📲 Get alerts on your phone

Watchlist hits can push straight to your phone — even when this app is closed. It uses ntfy, a free notification service. One-time setup:

  1. Install the free ntfy app (iPhone / Android)
  2. In the app, subscribe to your private topic below
  3. Tap Send test here — you should feel a buzz

Treat the topic name like a password — anyone who knows it can see your alerts. The scanner sends to this topic automatically once NTFY_TOPIC is set as a secret on the GitHub repo running the scheduled scans.
