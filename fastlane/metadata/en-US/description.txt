YardScout tracks the live inventory of self-service salvage yards across the country, so you stop driving to yards on a maybe.

FIND THE CAR BEFORE YOU DRIVE
- Live inventory from 250+ self-serve yards — Pick-n-Pull, LKQ Pick Your Part, Pull-A-Part and a growing set of independents — refreshed throughout the day
- Search by make, model and year range around your ZIP
- See the row number, arrival date and VIN before you leave the house
- Share any car as a link — yard, row, set-out date and how long cars usually last at that yard
- Model search understands yard naming quirks — searching WRX finds every spelling

FIND THE PART THAT FITS
- Name the part and your car; YardScout lists donor cars near you it fits, with how sure we are (Verified fit or Likely fit)
- Every car shows what it came from the factory with, so you know what to look for in the row
- Compare one part's price across every yard in range, from each yard's own published price list

KNOW THE YARDS
- Every tracked yard near you with distance, car counts and what arrived this week
- Each yard's pull-price list right in the app
- Tool rules per yard (sawzalls, compressors) so you bring the right gear
- Every yard's pull prices on the car cards, from its own published list

WATCH FOR THE RIGHT CAR
- Build a watchlist of makes, models and years — even a model that isn't in the rows yet
- Watched cars light up in the app when they arrive at a yard in your radius
- Free watches get a weekly recap push of every watched car that landed

YARDSCOUT PRO
- Price list before you drive: what the yard charges vs what the same used part goes for online, on nearly every car
- Donor-car alerts: a push within hours of a watched car hitting a yard in your radius, plus sale-day alerts
- Which parts get picked over fast, days on the lot and leaving-soon signals — get there before the crusher
- Unlimited radius and nationwide search

NO ACCOUNT, NO EMAIL
- Light and dark mode — follows your phone, or pick one
- The whole app works without signing up
- Alerts use an anonymous device ID — we never ask who you are

YardScout Pro is $8.99/month through your App Store account, auto-renews monthly, cancel anytime in Settings. Finding cars is free forever.
