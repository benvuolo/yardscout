📲 Get alerts on your phone

Watchlist hits can push straight to your phone — even when this app is closed. It uses ntfy, a free notification service. One-time setup:

  1. Install the free ntfy app (iPhone / Android)
  2. In the app, subscribe to your private topic below
  3. Tap Send test here — you should feel a buzz

Treat the topic name like a password — anyone who knows it can see your alerts. The scanner sends to this topic automatically once NTFY_TOPIC is set as a secret on the GitHub repo running the scheduled scans.

About the numbers: All dollar figures are ranges based on typical eBay sold prices (a national market) for used parts in working condition. Facebook Marketplace and local classifieds are local markets — prices vary by area, so check your own market before pulling. Condition dominates: a cracked, faded, or non-working part may be worth little or nothing, and none of these figures are a guarantee. Pull costs are shown only when they come from that yard's own published price list; otherwise you'll see “check yard price list” and the estimate is resale-only.

About coverage: Junkyard Hunter tracks every major self-service chain with online inventory — Pick-n-Pull, LKQ Pick Your Part, and Pull-A-Part (including former U-Pull-&-Pay yards) — ~150 yards nationwide, plus Utah's Tear-A-Part and Utah Pic-A-Part. Independent local yards aren't tracked yet.

Always inspect a part on the car before you buy. Inventory reflects the last scan and yards sell or crush cars daily.
