Common questions

A car I'm looking at isn't in the app

YardScout tracks self-service chains that publish inventory online. Independent local yards without online inventory aren't covered yet. Inventory refreshes several times a day, so very fresh arrivals can take a few hours to appear.

A price or part looks wrong

Pull costs come from each yard's published price list and resale ranges from recent national sold listings — both can drift. Email us the car and the part and we'll correct it.

Managing or canceling Pro

Subscriptions are handled by Apple: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → YardScout. Canceling keeps Pro until the end of the paid period. Refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Alerts stopped arriving

Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → YardScout is allowed, then toggle push off and on in the app's Alerts tab. Still stuck? Email us.
