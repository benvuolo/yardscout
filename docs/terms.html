YardScout is an informational tool for people who pull their own parts at self-service junkyards. Using it means you're okay with the following — written plainly, because that's the whole point of this app.

It's a research tool, not a promise

Everything YardScout shows — inventory, arrival dates, rows, part lists, value ranges, pull costs, demand speed, lot-lifespan estimates — is our best effort at useful information, not a guarantee. Value figures are ranges based on typical eBay sold prices for parts in working condition; a cracked, faded, or non-working part may be worth little or nothing. Local market prices vary. Nothing in the app is a promise of income, profit, or availability.

Inventory accuracy is best-effort

We re-scan the yards' published inventories on a schedule, but yards sell and crush cars daily, and yards track cars — not remaining parts. A car may be gone, and a listed part may already be pulled, by the time you visit. Always confirm at the yard. Pull costs come from each chain's published price list where available and can change without notice; the price at the register is the price.

Junkyards are industrial environments; follow each yard's safety rules.
