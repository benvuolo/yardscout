# Car pages (/car/<vin>) are share pages for one car on one lot: they exist
# so a link sent in a text or a thread shows the car, and they are gone when
# the car leaves. They are not for search crawlers: each one costs three
# requests on our side, and on 2026-11-14 a crawler walked 28,000 of them in
# an afternoon. Model, yard, city and price pages are the ones to index.
#
# Buttons on our own pages open the app with a source tag
# (/?utm_source=page&...): the same page as /, so not for crawling either.
